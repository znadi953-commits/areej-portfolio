6️⃣ FAQ (zidihom f section FAQ dyal la boutique)

Q: How do I receive my files?

Instantly after purchase! Etsy shows a download page right away and also emails you the links. Go to You → Purchases → Download Files.

Q: Can I print at home?

Yes! The PRINT version is formatted for standard A4/US Letter paper. The DIGITAL version works great on tablets (GoodNotes, Notability...).

Q: What age are the workbooks for?

Most workbooks suit ages 6–12; the Inner Critic and Screen Balance books also work beautifully for teens and adults.

Q: Can I use these in my classroom or therapy practice?

Personal and single-classroom use is welcome! For larger use, message me for a license.

Q: Will I receive anything by mail?

No — this is a digital product only. Nothing will be shipped.
